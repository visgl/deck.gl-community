This example applies CORTAO screen-space ambient occlusion to four test scenes: extruded city
buildings, terrain, procedural primitives, and bike parking counts as extruded A5 cells. Use the
panel to select the scene and the method, to show the occlusion only, to turn off the bilateral
filter, and to change the sample count.
