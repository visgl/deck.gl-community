# Third-party notices

`src/cortao-effect/` contains a port of CORTAO (Circular Occluder Ray-Traced Ambient Occlusion) by
Daniel Isheden.

- The shaders and the occluder lookup table generator are adapted from the CORTAO reference
  implementation: https://github.com/theagentd/CORTAO
- The default parameters follow the Carbon Engine version of CORTAO:
  https://github.com/carbonengine/CORTAO
