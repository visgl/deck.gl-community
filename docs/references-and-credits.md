# Attribution

deck.gl-community is developed by vis.gl contributors and builds on the deck.gl visualization
framework. The repository’s history records contributions to its packages, examples, and
documentation. [deck.gl](https://deck.gl/) · [Contributors](https://github.com/visgl/deck.gl-community/graphs/contributors)

## Project origins

- The wind layers and historical showcase build on Nicolas Belmonte’s original deck.gl wind
  visualization. The guide links to the source and describes the historical forecast data.
  [Wind showcase](./modules/geo-layers/developer-guide/wind-showcase.md)
- The Vega experiment retains its upstream acknowledgments and license notices in the module.
  [Vega credits](https://github.com/visgl/deck.gl-community/tree/master/modules/vega#attribution-and-upstream-project)

The [Community page](./community.md) explains how to report an issue or contribute.
